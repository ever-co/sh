Three ways to run an Ever product:

1. **Run it yourself** on your server or cluster. You keep everything; the install page gives you
   the settings.
2. **Ever Cloud**: Ever runs it for you. Create a workspace from your Ever account.
3. **Third-party hosts**: providers with a ready-made template or a documented method.

Whichever you choose, it is the same software.
