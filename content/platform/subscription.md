Self-hosting an Ever product is free, and the code is never locked behind a subscription.

An optional subscription adds services around your install, such as support, and Ever Platform
features that run on our side. Existing plans, limits and purchases keep working as they do today.

Current plans: [ever.co/pricing](https://ever.co/pricing).
