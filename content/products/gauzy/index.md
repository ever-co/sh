Ever Gauzy is an open business management platform: time tracking, HR, CRM, invoicing and more.
You can run all of it on infrastructure you control.

## Choose how to run it

- [Docker Compose](/gauzy/docker): one server, the fastest start.
- [Install settings](/install/gauzy): the environment block for your install, with the optional
  connection to your Ever account.
- [Other options](/hosting?product=gauzy): Kubernetes, Ever Cloud and third-party hosts.

Before you start, check the [requirements](/gauzy/requirements).

The complete product documentation is at [docs.gauzy.co](https://docs.gauzy.co), and the source
is on [GitHub](https://github.com/ever-co/ever-gauzy).
